How it works

  • Workload in Erlangs is calls × AHT ÷ 1,800 seconds.
  • For each interval the calculator tries agent counts upward until the Erlang C service level, 1 − C × e−(N−A)·T/AHT, meets the target and occupancy stays under the cap.
  • Scheduled heads divide required agents by (1 − shrinkage) to cover breaks, meetings, training and absence.
  • Erlang C assumes nobody hangs up, so it leans slightly towards overstaffing. That is usually the safer mistake.

Harbor & Pine Co. is a fictional company. All volumes and results are synthetic sample data.
