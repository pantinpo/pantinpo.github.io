AI prompt library for operations teams

15 prompts for the everyday work of an operations and customer care team: weekly reporting, customer replies, data cleanup, team updates and SOPs. Each one has a fill-in template, a worked example and tips, plus the ground rules that keep customer data out of AI tools.

Browse the prompts Download the Word version

The examples use Harbor & Pine Co., a fictional Australian home-goods store. All names and numbers are made up.

How to use it

  1. Copy a prompt and fill in every {placeholder}.
  2. Paste it into your AI assistant as one message. Tables go in as text, not screenshots.
  3. Check the output against your source: every number, name and date.
  4. If it's off, say what's wrong and ask again.

Ground rules

No customer personal data
Strip names, emails, phones, addresses and order numbers first.
No payment or login details
Never paste card numbers, bank details, passwords or keys.
A person checks everything
Nothing goes to a customer or a manager unread.
Numbers come from the source
Every figure in a report must match the original data.
Approved tools only
Use the team's approved assistant, not personal accounts.
It doesn't decide policy
It can draft the refund reply. It can't approve the refund.
