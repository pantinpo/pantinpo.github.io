# Operations starter kit

Three documents a small online business needs once its customer care and fulfilment teams grow past a handful of people. They're written for **Harbor & Pine Co.**, a fictional Australian home-goods store with a care team split between Melbourne and Manila. All names and numbers are made up.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `sop-refund-and-return-requests.docx` | SOP for refunds and returns: roles and RACI, a 9-step procedure with decision points, approval limits (A$150 / A$500), SLAs, escalation path, quality checks and common mistakes. |
| `new-team-member-onboarding-checklist.docx` | Onboarding checklist for a remote customer care hire: system access, day 1, week 1, 30/60/90-day goals, check-in cadence and sign-off. |
| `weekly-kpi-scorecard.xlsx` | 16 KPIs across Sales, Customer care, Fulfilment and Marketing. 8 weeks of actuals, automatic On track / Watch / Off track status, week-on-week change, a formula-built trend bar, and a summary tab that lists what needs attention. |

## Opening the files

**Google Docs:** upload the `.docx` to Google Drive, right-click it and choose *Open with > Google Docs*. The headings carry over, so the outline panel works straight away.

**Google Sheets:** upload the `.xlsx` to Drive and open it with Google Sheets, or go to *File > Import > Upload* in a new sheet. It only uses standard functions (IF, COUNTIF, COUNTIFS, INDEX, MATCH, MID, ROUND), so it behaves the same in Excel.

The scorecard's **Read me** tab explains the weekly update in five steps.
