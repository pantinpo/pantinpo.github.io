Google Sheets + Apps Script

Weekly business review robot

  • Apps Script
  • Google Sheets
  • Gmail
Problem
Every Monday someone spent the morning pasting store orders, helpdesk tickets and ad spend into one sheet, fixing formats by hand, and writing the same summary email.
What I built
A script that cleans all three messy exports, removes duplicates, works out last week's numbers against the week before and the 4-week average, flags anything unusual, and emails a PDF summary at 8am every Monday.
Result
The review lands before 8am with a short list of what moved and what to check first, and every run is written to a log.

Web tool + Excel / Google Sheets

Staffing calculator & WFM toolkit

  • Erlang C
  • Intraday RTA
  • Adherence
Problem
The care team was staffed on gut feel, so lunchtime queues blew out while the evenings sat idle.
What I built
An Erlang C calculator that turns forecast volume into agents needed for every half hour, plus an intraday sheet for real-time tracking against forecast and an adherence tracker for each agent.
Result
Change the service level target or the day's volume and the whole staffing plan updates in seconds. Anything off track during the day shows up red straight away, not in next week's report.

AI prompt library for ops teams

  • Prompt engineering
  • AI governance

What I built
A searchable library of 15 ready-to-use prompts for reporting, customer replies, data cleanup, team updates and SOP writing, each with an example and simple rules on what never goes into an AI tool.

Anyone on the team can copy a proven prompt in one click, and the safety rules come with it.
